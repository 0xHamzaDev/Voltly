Why it matters

A lithium battery ages faster when it is held at 100%, and faster still when it runs warm. A laptop that lives on the charger spends most of its life in both conditions. Voltly keeps the charge lower and backs off when the battery gets hot.

Questions, answered.

Is it safe for my battery?

Voltly only stops charging earlier than 100%. It does not push the battery beyond what macOS allows, and holding a lower charge is gentler on a lithium battery.

Why does it need my password?

Controlling charging means changing low-level power settings that only an administrator can change. On first launch Voltly asks for your admin password to install a small helper that controls charging.

Does the limit hold while the Mac sleeps?

Yes. On recent macOS versions the system keeps enforcing the limit while the Mac sleeps.

What happens when I quit Voltly?

Normal charging returns within seconds.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon running macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
